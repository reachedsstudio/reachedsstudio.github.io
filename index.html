Studio

About the studio

We believe good buildings are made slowly — through close reading of a site, honest materials and a patient attention to the details people touch every day.

Reached Studio works across private houses, cultural spaces and small public projects. Every commission is led directly by the studio's principals, from the first sketch through to the last site visit.

We collaborate closely with engineers, makers and landscape designers, and we prefer to stay small so that each project gets our full attention.

01

Site first

Orientation, climate and context set the terms. We draw the land before we draw the building.

02

Honest structure

Clear, legible structure and a restrained palette of materials that age gracefully.

03

Considered detail

Thresholds, handrails and window reveals — the small moments that make a space feel right.
